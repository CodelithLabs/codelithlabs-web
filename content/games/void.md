## What is VOID?

VOID is a free, browser-based 3D horror tunnel runner built by CodelithLabs.
Fly through an infinite procedurally generated tunnel. Dodge glowing red obstacles
that spawn faster as you survive longer.

## How to Play

Move with WASD / Arrow Keys, aim with your mouse, or swipe on touch screens.
Don't hit the red obstacles. Survive as long as possible.

## Privacy

VOID stores only one value on your device: your high score in `localStorage`.
Zero data is sent to any server. No account. No tracking. No cookies.

## Technical Details

Built with Three.js (WebGL), Next.js 14 App Router, and TypeScript.
Fully client-rendered. Works offline after first load. Available in 6 languages.

VOID is inspired by Project HollowMind — CodelithLabs' upcoming story-driven
psychological horror game for iOS, Android, and PC.
