## 📝 Best Practices

1. **Choose the right regime based on deductions** — If your Section 80C + 80D + home loan interest exceeds Rs. 2 lakh, the old regime typically saves more tax. If you have minimal deductions, the new regime's lower rates are better.
2. **Maximize Section 80C** — If sticking with old regime, ensure you invest the full Rs. 1.5 lakh limit in PPF, EPF, ELSS, or NSC to save Rs. 46,800 tax (assuming 30% slab + cess).
3. **Claim HRA correctly** — HRA exemption is least of: (a) actual HRA received, (b) rent paid minus 10% of salary, or (c) 50% of salary (metro) / 40% (non-metro). Use Form 16 and rent receipts when figuring.
4. **Plan advance tax payments** — If tax liability exceeds Rs. 10,000, pay advance tax in 4 installments (15th June, Sept, Dec, March) to avoid interest u/s 234B and 234C.
5. **File ITR even if no tax is due** — If your income exceeds basic exemption limit or you have TDS deducted, file ITR to claim refunds and maintain financial records for loans/visas.
6. **Declare all income sources** — Include interest income, rental income, capital gains from stocks/property, freelance income — all are taxable and need to be reported even if below taxable threshold.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Which tax regime should I choose in FY 2026-27?
Choose the old regime if your total deductions (Section 80C + 80D + home loan interest + HRA) exceed Rs. 2-2.5 lakh and you fall in the 20-30% tax bracket. Choose the new regime if you have minimal deductions or don't want the hassle of tax-saving investments. The calculator shows the exact tax under both regimes — pick the one with lower liability. You can switch regimes every year if you have only salary income.

### How is Section 80C deduction calculated?
Section 80C allows a maximum deduction of Rs. 1.5 lakh per financial year from taxable income. Eligible investments include PPF, EPF, ELSS mutual funds, life insurance premiums (up to 10% of sum assured), home loan principal repayment, NSC, Sukanya Samriddhi, and tuition fees. The total across all 80C instruments cannot exceed Rs. 1.5 lakh. For example, if you invest Rs. 1 lakh in PPF and Rs. 80,000 in EPF, you can claim only Rs. 1.5 lakh, not Rs. 1.8 lakh.

### How is HRA exemption calculated?
HRA exemption is the minimum of three amounts:  
(a) Actual HRA received from employer,  
(b) Rent paid minus 10% of basic salary,  
(c) 50% of basic salary if living in metro (Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata) or 40% for non-metro.  
For example, if your basic salary is Rs. 50,000/month (Rs. 6 lakh/year), HRA received is Rs. 20,000/month (Rs. 2.4 lakh/year), and rent paid is Rs. 15,000/month (Rs. 1.8 lakh/year), the exemption is minimum of: (a) Rs. 2.4L, (b) Rs. 1.8L - Rs. 60K = Rs. 1.2L, (c) Rs. 3L (metro). So Rs. 1.2 lakh is exempt from tax.
