# PPF Calculator India

Plan your long-term savings with the Public Provident Fund calculator. PPF is one of the safest and most tax-efficient investment options available to Indian residents, offering guaranteed returns backed by the Government of India. This calculator helps you estimate your maturity value based on your annual contribution, the current interest rate, and the investment tenure. See exactly how your corpus grows year by year, including the total amount deposited and the total interest earned over the full 15-year term or any extended duration.

## 🚀 Features

- **Maturity Value Projection** — Calculate the final PPF corpus based on annual deposits and the prevailing interest rate
- **Year-by-Year Growth Table** — View opening balance, deposit, interest earned, and closing balance for each year of the PPF tenure
- **Flexible Deposit Amounts** — Enter fixed annual deposits or vary the amount each year to model real-world contribution patterns
- **Interest Rate Adjustment** — Default set to the current 7.1% rate with the ability to test different rates for scenario planning
- **Extension Period Modeling** — Calculate returns for the standard 15-year lock-in plus 5-year extension blocks
- **Tax Savings Display** — See the Section 80C deduction benefit (up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per year) alongside your maturity projection
- **Indian Number Formatting** — All values shown in lakhs and crores with the rupee symbol

## 📖 How to Use PPF Calculator

1. **Enter Annual Deposit** — Type the amount you plan to invest each year (minimum Rs. 500, maximum Rs. 1.5 lakh per annum as per PPF rules).
2. **Set Interest Rate** — The default rate is 7.1% (Q1 FY2026-27 rate). Adjust this if you want to test different scenarios or if the rate has been revised.
3. **Choose Tenure** — Select the standard 15-year lock-in period, or extend to 20, 25, or 30 years in 5-year blocks.
4. **Select Deposit Frequency** — Choose whether you deposit once a year or in monthly installments (the calculator applies interest accordingly).
5. **View Results** — Review the maturity value, total deposits, total interest earned, and the year-by-year breakdown table.
6. **Compare Scenarios** — Change the deposit amount or rate to see how different assumptions affect your final corpus.

## 💡 Common Use Cases

### Retirement Corpus Building
PPF is a cornerstone of conservative retirement planning in India. If you invest Rs. 1.5 lakh annually at 7.1% for 15 years, your maturity value will be approximately Rs. 40.7 lakh — of which Rs. 18.2 lakh is pure interest income, completely tax-free. Many investors continue beyond 15 years in 5-year extension blocks to further grow the corpus.

### Tax-Free Wealth Accumulation
PPF enjoys EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status under Indian tax law. Contributions qualify for Section 80C deduction (up to Rs. 1.5 lakh), the interest earned is tax-free, and the maturity amount is entirely tax-free. Use this calculator to quantify the tax advantage compared to taxable fixed deposits or other investments.

### Children's Education Planning
Parents often open PPF accounts for children as a long-term savings vehicle. Starting early with even Rs. 50,000 per year can accumulate a meaningful education fund by the time the child reaches college age. The guaranteed nature of returns makes PPF ideal for non-negotiable financial goals.

### Conservative Portfolio Allocation
Financial advisors recommend allocating a portion of the portfolio to risk-free instruments. PPF serves as the debt anchor in a balanced portfolio. Use this calculator alongside equity projections to plan your overall asset allocation strategy.

## 🔧 Technical Details

### Processing Technology
- Interest is compounded annually as per PPF rules (interest is calculated on the lowest balance between the 5th and the last day of each month, credited at year-end)
- The formula for maturity value with fixed annual deposits: F = P x [((1+r)^n - 1) / r] where P is annual deposit, r is the annual interest rate, and n is the number of years
- For monthly deposits, the calculator models monthly interest accrual per PPF accounting rules
- All computation runs in client-side JavaScript

### Supported Formats
- Inputs: Annual or monthly deposit amounts (Rs. 500 to Rs. 1,50,000 per year), interest rate, tenure in years
- Outputs: Maturity value, total deposits, total interest, year-by-year schedule

## 📝 Best Practices

1. **Deposit before the 5th of each month** — PPF interest is calculated on the minimum balance between the 5th and the end of the month. Depositing before the 5th ensures your money earns interest for that month.
2. **Invest the maximum Rs. 1.5 lakh for full Section 80C benefit** — If your budget allows, investing the maximum amount each year gives you the best combination of guaranteed returns and tax savings.
3. **Use lump sum at the start of the financial year** — Investing the full annual amount in April earns interest for all 12 months, resulting in a higher maturity value compared to spreading deposits across the year.
4. **Plan for the lock-in period** — PPF has a 15-year lock-in with limited partial withdrawal allowed from the 7th year. Ensure you do not need this money before the lock-in expires.
5. **Review the interest rate quarterly** — The Government of India revises PPF rates every quarter. Check the latest rate at the start of each quarter for accurate projections.
6. **Consider extensions for compounding benefit** — After 15 years, extending in 5-year blocks with continued deposits significantly boosts your corpus due to compounding on a much larger base.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the current PPF interest rate in 2026?
As of Q1 FY2026-27 (April-June 2026), the PPF interest rate stands at 7.1% per annum, compounded annually. This rate has remained unchanged since January 2023. The Government of India reviews and announces PPF rates every quarter based on the yield of government securities. Always verify the latest rate on the RBI or Ministry of Finance website before making investment decisions.

### Can I extend my PPF account beyond 15 years?
Yes, you can extend your PPF account in blocks of 5 years after the initial 15-year maturity. You have two options: (1) Extension with deposits — continue contributing up to Rs. 1.5 lakh annually and earn interest on the entire balance, or (2) Extension without deposits — stop contributing but let the existing balance continue earning interest. The extension request must be submitted within one year of maturity.

### Is PPF interest taxable in India?
No. PPF enjoys EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt) status under the Income Tax Act. This means contributions up to Rs. 1.5 lakh per year qualify for deduction under Section 80C, the interest earned each year is completely tax-free, and the maturity amount is entirely exempt from income tax. This makes PPF one of the most tax-efficient long-term investment options available to Indian residents. There is no TDS on PPF interest, regardless of the amount.
