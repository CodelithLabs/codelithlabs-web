# Text to Speech

Convert text to natural-sounding speech using your browser's built-in voices.

## 🚀 Features

- **100% Client-Side Processing** - Your data never leaves your browser
- **Multiple Voices** - Choose from your browser's available voice options
- **Speed Control** - Adjust speech rate to your preference
- **Pitch Control** - Fine-tune voice pitch
- **Pause & Resume** - Full playback control
- **No Sign-Up Required** - Start using immediately
- **Privacy-First** - Zero data collection or storage
- **Completely Free** - No hidden costs or premium tiers

## 📖 How to Use Text to Speech

1. **Open the Tool**: Navigate to the Text to Speech tool on CodelithLabs
2. **Enter Your Text**: Paste or type the text you want spoken
3. **Select Voice**: Choose from available voices and languages
4. **Adjust Settings**: Set speed, pitch, and volume
5. **Play**: Click play to hear your text read aloud
