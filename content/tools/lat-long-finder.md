# Latitude & Longitude Finder

Find coordinates for any address or get your current location's coordinates.

## 🚀 Features

- **100% Client-Side Processing** - Your data never leaves your browser
- **Address to Coordinates** - Geocode any address to lat/long

- **Copy Formats** - Copy as DD, DMS, or comma-separated
- **Reverse Geocode** - Convert coordinates back to address
- **No Sign-Up Required** - Start using immediately
