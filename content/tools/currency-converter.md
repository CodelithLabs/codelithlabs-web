## 📝 Best Practices

1. **Check rates before large transfers** — Exchange rates fluctuate hourly. For transfers over ₹5 lakhs, even 0.5% makes a meaningful difference.
2. **Compare with your bank's rate** — Banks add 1-3% markup over mid-market. Our rate helps you see how much markup you're paying.
3. **Consider transfer fees** — The exchange rate is only part of the cost. Wire transfers, service fees, and GST on forex purchases add up.
4. **Time major conversions** — If possible, convert when the rate is favorable. Track trends over the past week using the historical chart.
5. **Use for tax calculations** — The Income Tax Department of India accepts the SBI reference rate for forex income reporting. Our rates approximate this but use official rates for filing.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these rates the same as my bank offers?
No. Banks and money transfer services add a markup (spread) of 1-5% over the mid-market rate. Our tool shows the mid-market rate so you can see exactly how much your bank is charging above the real rate.

### How often are rates updated?
Rates update hourly. For real-time trading rates, use dedicated forex platforms. Our rates are ideal for budgeting, invoicing, and general comparison.

### Why does Google show a different rate?
Rate differences between sources are normal (0.01-0.1%). Different providers aggregate from different data sources and update at different intervals. For practical purposes, the difference is negligible.

### Can I convert cryptocurrency?
We support BTC, ETH, and other major cryptocurrencies. Crypto rates are more volatile and may differ between exchanges. Our rate represents a cross-exchange average.

### Is this suitable for accounting and invoicing?
For general invoicing and budgeting, yes. For statutory compliance (tax filing, customs declarations), use the official RBI reference rate published daily at 1:30 PM IST.
