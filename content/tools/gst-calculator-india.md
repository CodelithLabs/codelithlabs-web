## 🔧 Technical Details

### Processing Technology
- GST is calculated using straightforward percentage arithmetic applied to the input amount
- For "Add GST": Tax = Amount x (Rate / 100), Total = Amount + Tax
- For "Remove GST": Base = Total / (1 + Rate / 100), Tax = Total - Base
- CGST and SGST are each half of the total GST for intra-state; IGST equals full GST for inter-state
- All computations run in client-side JavaScript

## 📝 Best Practices

1. **Verify the GST slab for your product** — Different products fall under different slabs. Check the HSN/SAC code classification on the official GST portal before applying a rate.
2. **Use intra-state vs inter-state correctly** — Intra-state (within the same state) attracts CGST + SGST. Inter-state (across states) attracts IGST. Misclassification leads to incorrect filings.
3. **Account for cess separately** — Items like tobacco, coal, and luxury cars attract additional cess on top of GST. Always add cess where applicable to ensure accurate invoicing.
4. **Round to the nearest rupee for invoicing** — While the calculator shows paisa-level precision, most invoicing standards round tax components to the nearest rupee per line item.
5. **Cross-check with your accounting software** — Use this calculator as a quick verification tool, not a replacement for your primary accounting system.
6. **Stay updated on rate changes** — The GST Council periodically revises slab rates. Confirm that you are using the current rate for your product or service category.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I calculate GST from total amount?
To extract GST from a GST-inclusive total, divide the total amount by (1 + GST rate/100). For example, if the total is Rs. 1,180 and the GST rate is 18%, the base price is 1180 / 1.18 = Rs. 1,000. The GST component is Rs. 180. Use the "Remove GST" mode in the calculator to do this automatically with CGST/SGST or IGST split.

### What is the difference between CGST, SGST, and IGST?
CGST (Central GST) and SGST (State GST) are levied together on intra-state transactions, each being half the total GST rate. For example, an 18% GST slab means 9% CGST + 9% SGST. IGST (Integrated GST) is levied on inter-state transactions and equals the full GST rate. For cross-state sales at 18%, the entire 18% goes as IGST to the central government, which then settles with the destination state.

### Which GST slab applies to my product?
GST slabs in India are 0% (essential items like fresh food, milk), 5% (packaged food, footwear under Rs. 500), 12% (processed food, furniture), 18% (most services, electronics, capital goods), and 28% (luxury items, cars, aerated beverages). The exact classification depends on the HSN code (for goods) or SAC code (for services). Check the latest classification on the official GST portal at cbic-gst.gov.in or consult your tax advisor.
