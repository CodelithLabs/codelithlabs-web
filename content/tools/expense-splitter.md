# Expense Splitter

Split bills equally among friends with automatic settlement calculation.

## 🚀 Features

- **100% Client-Side Processing** - Your data never leaves your browser
- **Equal Splitting** - Divide expenses among any number of people
- **Settlement Optimization** - Minimizes number of transactions needed
- **Multiple Expenses** - Add multiple expenses in one session
- **Per-Person Breakdown** - See what each person owes or is owed
- **No Sign-Up Required** - Start using immediately
- **Privacy-First** - Zero data collection or storage
- **Completely Free** - No hidden costs or premium tiers

## 📖 How to Use Expense Splitter

1. **Open the Tool**: Navigate to the Expense Splitter on CodelithLabs
2. **Add Participants**: Enter the names of people involved
3. **Add Expenses**: Enter each expense with who paid and the amount
4. **Calculate**: Click calculate to see the settlement
5. **View Results**: See who owes whom and exact amounts
