Shared expenses cause conflict when rules are unclear. Fair systems are transparent, repeatable, and adapted to context.

## Common Split Methods

- equal split
- proportional-to-income split
- usage-based split
- hybrid split by category

## Choosing the Right Model

- equal split for equal usage contexts
- income-weighted split for mixed earning households
- usage-based for utilities/consumption variation

## FAQ

### Is equal split always fair?
Not always. Fairness depends on context and capacity.

### Should couples use income-based splits?
Often useful when incomes differ significantly.

### How often should split rules be reviewed?
Quarterly or after major life changes.

## Final Take

Money harmony comes from explicit rules, not assumptions. Pick a method, document it, and review it regularly.
