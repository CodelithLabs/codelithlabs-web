Rent vs buy debates are usually emotional. Good decisions are mathematical plus lifestyle-aware.

## Cost Components for Buying

- Down payment
- Loan interest cost
- Taxes, insurance, maintenance
- Transaction costs
- Opportunity cost of locked capital

## Cost Components for Renting

- Monthly rent outflow
- Annual rent escalation
- Potential investment of down payment elsewhere

## Break-Even Lens

Buying may win when you stay long enough and property appreciation plus principal payoff outweigh renting + invested alternatives.

## Flexibility Lens

Renting offers mobility and lower commitment. Buying offers ownership stability and potential long-term equity.

## Common Decision Errors

- Ignoring maintenance and transaction costs
- Comparing rent only to EMI (missing full ownership cost)
- Underestimating career/location uncertainty

## FAQ

### Is buying always better long term?
Not always. Outcomes depend on tenure, appreciation, and opportunity cost assumptions.

### What is the most important variable?
How long you realistically stay in the property.

### Should I max out affordability?
Usually no. Keep buffer for emergencies and lifestyle stability.

## Final Take

There is no universal winner. Build scenario models and decide with both numbers and life plans in mind.
