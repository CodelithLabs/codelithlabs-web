Capital gains tax can materially change your real returns. Two investments with identical gross gains can produce very different net outcomes depending on holding period and jurisdictional rules.

## Core Concepts

- **Capital gain:** selling price minus cost basis (adjusted for fees where applicable)
- **Short-term gains:** often taxed at higher ordinary rates
- **Long-term gains:** often taxed more favorably

## Why Holding Period Matters

The same asset sold a few months later can move into a different tax treatment band.

## Practical Planning Ideas

- Evaluate net, not gross return
- Consider tax-loss harvesting where legal
- Avoid panic selling without tax impact modeling

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring fees in cost basis
- Forgetting currency conversion impact for global assets
- Making decisions only on price movement, not net after-tax return

## FAQ

### Is long-term always better?
Not always. Portfolio goals and risk conditions still matter.

### Can losses reduce tax liability?
In many jurisdictions, yes, subject to local rules.

### Should tax decide every trade?
Tax matters, but portfolio strategy and risk management come first.

## Final Take

Tax-aware investing is return optimization, not just compliance. Model exits before executing them.
