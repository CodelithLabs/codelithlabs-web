Refinancing can reduce interest cost and monthly EMI, but only if total savings exceed fees and fit your holding timeline.

## What to Compare

- current interest rate vs new rate
- remaining tenure
- processing and legal costs
- prepayment/closure penalties

## Break-Even Logic

Break-even months = total refinance cost ÷ monthly savings.

If you may move/sell before break-even, refinance may not be worth it.

## Common Refinancing Goals

- lower monthly cash burden
- reduce total interest outflow
- switch from variable to fixed rate or vice versa

## FAQ

### Is any rate drop enough to refinance?
No. Fees and time horizon determine real benefit.

### Should I shorten tenure when refinancing?
If cash flow allows, it can materially reduce total interest.

### Can refinance hurt credit profile?
It may involve credit checks and new obligations; plan timing carefully.

## Final Take

Refinancing is a math decision first, convenience decision second. Always calculate break-even before signing.
