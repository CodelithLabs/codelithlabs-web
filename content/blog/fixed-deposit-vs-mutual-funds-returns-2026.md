Fixed deposits and mutual funds serve different investor needs. One prioritizes stability and predictability, the other targets growth with market-linked variability.

## Fixed Deposits: Strengths and Limits

**Strengths:** capital stability, predictable returns, simple structure.

**Limits:** lower long-term growth potential and inflation risk.

## Mutual Funds: Strengths and Limits

**Strengths:** diversification, growth potential, professional management.

**Limits:** market volatility and outcome uncertainty.

## Decision Framework

Choose based on:
- time horizon
- risk tolerance
- liquidity needs
- income vs growth objective

A blended portfolio often works best.

## FAQ

### Are fixed deposits risk-free?
No instrument is fully risk-free, but many FDs are lower-risk compared to equity funds.

### Can mutual funds beat FDs long term?
They may, especially equity-oriented funds over long periods, but with volatility.

### Should beginners avoid mutual funds?
Not necessarily—allocation and horizon matter more than labels.

## Final Take

Use fixed deposits for stability goals and mutual funds for growth goals. Portfolio design should reflect your personal risk capacity and timeline.
