Open Graph metadata controls how your links appear on social and messaging platforms. Weak previews can waste high-quality content by reducing click confidence.

## Core OG Elements

- `og:title`
- `og:description`
- `og:image`
- `og:url`
- `og:type`

## Why OG Is a Growth Lever

People decide in seconds whether to click a shared link. A strong preview improves trust and context instantly.

## Best Practices

1. Use clear, benefit-oriented titles
2. Keep description concise and specific
3. Ensure image dimensions are platform-friendly
4. Keep OG URL canonical and stable

## FAQ

### Do OG tags affect Google ranking directly?
Not directly, but they can improve distribution performance via social CTR.

### Should OG image match page hero image?
Usually yes for consistency, but campaign-specific variants can work.

### Why are my link previews wrong?
Often due to cache persistence or missing/invalid OG tags.

## Final Take

Open Graph is conversion infrastructure for social traffic. Treat it as a first-class publishing step.
