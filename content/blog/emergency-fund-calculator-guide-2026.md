An emergency fund is financial shock absorption. Without it, unexpected expenses usually force high-interest debt or panic withdrawals from long-term investments.

## How Much to Keep

Typical guidance:
- stable income: 3-6 months of essential expenses
- variable income/self-employed: 6-12 months

## Where to Park It

- high-liquidity low-risk instruments
- avoid locking emergency funds in volatile assets

## Build Strategy

1. calculate essential monthly burn
2. define target buffer range
3. automate monthly contributions
4. refill after withdrawals before discretionary investing

## FAQ

### Is emergency fund separate from investments?
Yes. Different purpose, different liquidity/risk profile.

### Should I pause investing to build emergency fund?
Often yes until a baseline buffer is established.

### Can I use credit cards as emergency fund?
That is debt capacity, not cash safety.

## Final Take

Emergency funds protect your long-term plan from short-term disruption. Build it before optimizing advanced portfolio strategy.
