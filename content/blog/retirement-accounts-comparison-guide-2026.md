Retirement planning is mostly about consistency and tax-efficient compounding over decades.

## Core Selection Criteria

- contribution limits
- tax treatment now vs later
- withdrawal flexibility
- investment options available

## A Practical Allocation Approach

1. maximize employer-matched plans first
2. diversify account tax treatment
3. automate contributions monthly
4. rebalance with age/risk profile changes

## Common Mistakes

- chasing recent returns
- underestimating inflation
- delaying contributions despite available cash flow

## FAQ

### Should I prioritize tax-deferred or tax-free accounts?
Usually a mix provides flexibility across uncertain future tax regimes.

### How often should I review retirement allocation?
At least annually or after major life/market changes.

### Is conservative allocation always safer?
Not necessarily—insufficient growth can also be a long-term risk.

## Final Take

Retirement success comes from disciplined contributions, tax-aware account mix, and long-horizon decision-making.
