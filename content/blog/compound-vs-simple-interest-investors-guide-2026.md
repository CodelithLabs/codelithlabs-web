Compound interest is often called the eighth wonder of the world for a reason: it makes time your strongest multiplier. Simple interest is easier to model, but less powerful for long-term growth.

## The Formula Difference

- **Simple interest:** principal grows linearly
- **Compound interest:** returns generate returns (exponential effect)

Over long periods, compounding usually creates dramatically larger outcomes.

## Where You’ll See Each

- Simple interest: short-term borrowing, basic lending structures
- Compound interest: savings, investments, most portfolio growth models

## Why Compounding Changes Decisions

Small differences in rate and time produce massive differences in final value. Delaying investment start by a few years can reduce outcomes significantly.

## Common Mistakes

- Ignoring compounding frequency
- Comparing nominal instead of effective annual rates
- Underestimating inflation impact on real returns

## FAQ

### Is compound interest always better?
For long-term growth, usually yes. For short fixed-tenure scenarios, simple models may be sufficient.

### Does compounding help debt too?
It can work against borrowers when debt compounds.

### What matters more: rate or time?
Both, but time is often the hidden superpower.

## Final Take

If your horizon is long, compounding should shape your strategy. Start early, stay consistent, and review assumptions regularly.
