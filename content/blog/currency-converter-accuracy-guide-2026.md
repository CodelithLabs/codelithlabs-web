Currency conversion is more than exchange rates shown on screen. Final conversion outcomes depend on spreads, platform fees, settlement timing, and transfer method.

## What Affects Conversion Accuracy

- base rate source and refresh frequency
- provider spread over benchmark rate
- fixed and percentage transfer fees
- conversion timing and volatility

## Practical Decision Steps

1. compare total payable amount, not just displayed rate
2. check hidden fees and card markups
3. monitor timing for large conversions

## FAQ

### Why does final amount differ from quoted rate?
Spreads, fees, and settlement timing differences.

### Is interbank rate available to all users?
Usually not directly; consumer services apply margins.

### Should I convert in advance or at payment time?
Depends on volatility, fees, and cash-flow constraints.

## Final Take

For cross-border finance, rate transparency and fee awareness are essential. Always compare total landed cost before converting.
