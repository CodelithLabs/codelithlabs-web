## EMI Planning Is Where Most Borrowers Win or Lose

A loan is not just about approval—it is about affordability over years. A difference of a few thousand rupees per month can affect savings, stress, and long-term wealth creation.

That is why a loan EMI calculator should be your first step before applying.

## What EMI Depends On

Your monthly EMI is driven by three inputs:

1. Principal amount
2. Interest rate
3. Loan tenure

If you reduce any one of these smartly, your monthly burden can improve significantly.

## Fast Pre-Loan Workflow (India)

### Step 1: Find your comfort EMI zone

Use the calculator to find a monthly amount that still leaves room for emergency savings and investments.

### Step 2: Compare multiple tenures

Shorter tenure = higher EMI but lower total interest.
Longer tenure = lower EMI but higher total interest.

### Step 3: Stress-test with higher interest

Test your EMI with a +1% interest scenario. This protects you from future rate resets.

## Example Thinking Framework

Before finalizing:

- Keep emergency buffer intact
- Ensure monthly EMI + fixed expenses is manageable
- Keep room for insurance + SIPs
- Avoid taking maximum eligible loan just because bank offers it

## Top Mistakes Borrowers Make

- Choosing tenure only by lowest EMI
- Ignoring total interest paid over full tenure
- Skipping prepayment planning
- Not accounting for job/income volatility
- Borrowing based on approval limit, not affordability

## Should You Prepay?

If your loan has high interest and no heavy prepayment penalties, periodic prepayments can reduce total interest burden. Even small annual prepayments can meaningfully reduce tenure.

Use calculations yearly to choose between:

- Extra EMI prepayment
- Additional investments
- A balanced split

## FAQ

### Is a longer tenure always better?

Only for monthly cash flow. Total interest outgo is generally higher over longer tenures.

### Can I use EMI calculators for personal loans too?

Yes. The same principal-rate-tenure model applies.

### How much of salary should go to EMI?

There is no universal rule, but keep enough room for savings, insurance, and unexpected costs.

### Should I invest or prepay loan first?

It depends on loan rate, return expectations, and risk comfort. Model both scenarios before deciding.
