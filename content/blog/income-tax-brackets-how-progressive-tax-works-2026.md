Many people think crossing into a higher tax bracket means all income is taxed at that higher rate. That is incorrect in progressive systems.

## How Progressive Tax Works

Income is split into bracket portions. Each portion is taxed at its bracket rate. Only the incremental amount gets the higher rate.

## FAQ

### Does a higher bracket reduce my total income suddenly?
No. Only the income slice in that bracket is taxed at the higher rate.

### Why is my effective rate lower than top bracket?
Because lower income portions are taxed at lower rates.

### Should I optimize taxes every month?
Quarterly reviews are often practical unless your income is highly variable.
