## Why Compound Interest Matters More Than Timing the Market

Most investors focus on finding the “perfect” entry point. In reality, long-term outcomes are usually driven more by consistency, compounding, and time in the market.

A simple compound interest calculator can show how small monthly investments grow into meaningful wealth over 10–30 years.

## The Formula in Plain English

Compound interest means your returns earn returns. Over time, growth accelerates because each cycle builds on a larger base.

What determines your final corpus:

- Initial investment
- Monthly contribution
- Expected annual return
- Time horizon
- Compounding frequency

If one variable does the heavy lifting, it is usually **time**.

## How to Use a Compound Interest Calculator Correctly

### 1. Use realistic return assumptions

Avoid using only optimistic return scenarios. Test conservative, base, and optimistic cases.

### 2. Include regular monthly contributions

Most real portfolios grow through recurring investments, not only one-time lumpsums.

### 3. Model inflation separately

A future corpus sounds big, but purchasing power changes. Always compare nominal vs real outcomes.

### 4. Revisit annually

Life changes, income changes, goals change. Update assumptions every year.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- Starting late despite having capacity to start now
- Stopping SIPs during volatility
- Ignoring emergency fund before aggressive investing
- Using one return assumption for all assets
- Not aligning horizon with goal (education/home/retirement)
