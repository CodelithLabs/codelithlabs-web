AI color tools can accelerate design exploration dramatically, but quality still depends on constraints and validation.

## Where AI Palettes Help Most

- Early concept exploration
- Rapid variant generation
- Brand moodboard creation
- Dark/light theme ideation

## Where Human Judgment Is Essential

- Accessibility contrast decisions
- Brand identity consistency
- Cross-platform visual testing

## Practical Design Workflow

1. Generate candidate sets in [AI Color Palette](/tools/ai-color-palette)
2. Normalize formats with [Color Converter](/tools/color-converter)
3. Validate implementation shades using [Color Picker](/tools/color-picker)

## Accessibility Baseline

- Maintain readable contrast for text/background pairs
- Test key components under both themes
- Avoid relying solely on color for status meaning

## FAQ

### Can AI replace visual designers?
No. It accelerates exploration but does not replace product judgment.

### Are generated palettes production-ready?
Usually not without accessibility and brand checks.

### Should one palette fit all UI states?
No. Define semantic tokens for success, warning, danger, and neutral states.

## Final Take

AI palettes are high-leverage when used as a starting point, not final truth. Pair speed with systematic accessibility and brand governance.
