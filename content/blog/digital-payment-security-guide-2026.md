Digital payments are convenient but fraud techniques evolve quickly. Strong payment security requires both user behavior and product-level controls.

## Top Threats in 2026

- phishing and fake payment pages
- SIM-swap based account takeover
- social engineering in refund workflows
- credential stuffing on weak accounts

## User-Side Protection Basics

1. enable MFA
2. avoid unknown links for payment
3. verify merchant identity
4. monitor transaction alerts actively

## Team-Side Security Controls

- device and risk scoring
- anomaly detection for velocity spikes
- secure webhook verification
- robust dispute workflows

## FAQ

### Is OTP enough for payment safety?
OTP helps, but layered controls are needed against modern attacks.

### What is the highest-impact team control?
Real-time anomaly detection plus secure authentication.

### Should users save cards everywhere?
Only on trusted platforms with strong account security.

## Final Take

Payment security is a shared responsibility. Build layered defenses and make secure behavior the easiest path for users.
