Sitemaps are discovery accelerators, especially for large or frequently updated sites. They help search engines find and prioritize URLs efficiently.

## What a Good Sitemap Includes

- canonical indexable URLs only
- accurate `lastmod` values
- clean segmentation when URL volume grows
- no blocked/noindex mismatch entries

## Workflow Principles

1. generate automatically from source of truth
2. exclude draft/redirect/noindex pages
3. validate format and URL status
4. monitor indexing deltas over time

## FAQ

### Do sitemaps guarantee indexing?
No, but they improve discovery and crawl efficiency.

### Should low-value pages be in sitemap?
Usually no. Include URLs you want indexed.

### How often should I update sitemap?
Whenever indexable URL sets or major timestamps change.

## Final Take

Treat sitemaps as operational SEO infrastructure: automated, validated, and monitored.
