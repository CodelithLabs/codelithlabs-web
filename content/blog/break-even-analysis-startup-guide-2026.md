Break-even analysis tells you when your business stops burning cash on core operations. For founders, this is not an accounting exercise—it is survival intelligence.

## Core Formula

Break-even units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price per Unit − Variable Cost per Unit)

This gives a decision baseline for pricing, hiring, and marketing spend.

## Why Founders Get It Wrong

- Underestimating variable costs
- Ignoring support and payment-processing costs
- Assuming unrealistic conversion rates
- Confusing revenue growth with profitability

## High-Impact Use Cases

- Pricing experiment planning
- Sales target setting
- Product line rationalization
- Channel profitability comparisons

## FAQ

### Is break-even enough for financial planning?
No. It is a baseline metric; cash flow and runway analysis are also critical.

### Can break-even improve pricing decisions?
Yes, it reveals minimum sustainable pricing boundaries.

### Should early startups chase break-even immediately?
Depends on strategy, funding, and growth model.

## Final Take

Break-even analysis is one of the fastest ways to improve founder decision quality. Use it continuously, not once per quarter.
