Lorem ipsum is useful for quick mockups, but it often hides real content risks. Teams discover layout breaks only after production copy arrives.

## Why Traditional Placeholder Text Fails

- It does not represent true word length variability
- It hides localization expansion issues
- It misses realistic tone and semantic hierarchy

## Better Placeholder Strategies

1. Use domain-specific sample text
2. Include short and long variants
3. Simulate multilingual expansion
4. Test with real headlines and CTA verbs

## For Product Teams

Design quality improves when content, design, and engineering collaborate earlier. Placeholder strategy is a small habit with high downstream impact.

## FAQ

### Is lorem ipsum still useful?
Yes for early wireframes, but insufficient for realistic validation.

### Should I include localization checks in design stage?
Yes, especially for global products.

### Do placeholders affect SEO work?
They can, if they delay final content quality checks until late.

## Final Take

Use lorem ipsum intentionally, then replace it early with realistic content to avoid UX surprises and costly late-stage fixes.
