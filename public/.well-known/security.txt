# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

If you discover a security vulnerability within CodelithLabs, please send an email to team.codelithlabs@gmail.com
. All security vulnerabilities will be promptly addressed.

## Scope

This security policy applies to:
- codelithlabs.in domain and all subdomains
- All tools and utilities hosted on our platform
- Client-side processing code and web workers
- Any third-party integrations

## Out of Scope

- Theoretical vulnerabilities without proof of concept
- Social engineering attacks against CodelithLabs users
- Physical attacks against CodelithLabs infrastructure
- Denial of Service attacks

## Safe Harbor

CodelithLabs supports safe harbor for security researchers who:
- Make a good faith effort to avoid privacy violations and data destruction
- Give us reasonable time to respond before public disclosure
- Do not violate any other applicable laws or regulations

We will not pursue legal action against researchers who follow these guidelines.

## Acknowledgments

We appreciate the work of security researchers and will acknowledge verified reports (with permission) on our website.

# Thank you for helping keep CodelithLabs and our users safe!
